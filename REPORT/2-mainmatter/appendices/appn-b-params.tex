\chapter{Per-Experiment Policy Parameters}
\label{appn:params}

This appendix records the exact policy parameters used in the E0 and E3
experiments.  All parameters are taken from the run directory
\texttt{outputs/experiments\_20260424\_132309/}.

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{E0 --- Analytical Baseline}

E0 uses newsvendor base-stock levels computed analytically from demand
statistics and lead times.  No dispatch threshold is set (all edges use
\texttt{min\_dispatch\_utilization = 0.0}).

\begin{verbatim}
note: "Analytical newsvendor base-stock levels, no dispatch threshold"
\end{verbatim}

Exact per-node, per-SKU base-stock levels are read directly from the
config at runtime and are documented in \texttt{config/1n3\_5sku.json}.

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{E3 --- Joint Optimisation (Headline Result)}

Base-stock levels (integers) and dispatch thresholds (fractions)
optimised by Optuna TPE across 100 trials with a 92\% fill-rate
constraint.  Best feasible trial: \#27.

The supplier-to-warehouse thresholds are high ($\sim$0.80), consolidating
nearly full trucks on the long-haul leg.  The warehouse-to-retailer
thresholds are moderate ($\sim$0.23--0.39), allowing more frequent
smaller deliveries to maintain retailer service levels.

Full machine-readable parameters are in
\texttt{outputs/experiments\_20260424\_132309/E3/params.json}.
