\chapter{Test Suite Summary}
\label{appn:tests}

The framework is validated by approximately 80 pytest tests in the
\texttt{tests/} directory, organised into 21 test modules.  Tests are
run with \texttt{python -m pytest tests/ -q}.

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{Test Modules by Category}

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Test modules and their coverage areas.}
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lp{9cm}@{}}
    \toprule
    Module & Coverage \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{test\_deterministic\_constant\_demand} &
      Base-stock convergence under constant demand; confirms
      on-hand reaches target within $L+1$ days. \\
    \texttt{test\_deterministic\_no\_demand} &
      Simulator does not place orders or accumulate costs when
      demand is zero. \\
    \texttt{test\_zero\_lead\_time\_not\_lost} &
      Goods ordered with lead time 0 arrive same-day; no
      units are silently dropped. \\
    \texttt{test\_negative\_demand\_clipped} &
      Negative demand values are clipped to zero; inventory
      does not increase from demand. \\
    \texttt{test\_no\_negative\_on\_hand} &
      On-hand inventory never goes below zero across all
      demand and policy configurations. \\
    \texttt{test\_costs\_and\_kpis} &
      Holding, transport, ordering, and shortage costs
      accumulate correctly; warm-up exclusion verified. \\
    \texttt{test\_inventory\_recovery} &
      After a demand spike, inventory recovers to base-stock
      within the expected number of lead-time periods. \\
    \texttt{test\_local\_flow\_conservation} &
      Units entering the network equal units leaving plus
      units stored (flow conservation). \\
    \texttt{test\_policy\_behavior} &
      Policy \texttt{order\_qty} returns correct values for
      each of the seven policy types across edge cases. \\
    \texttt{test\_ss\_policy\_behavior} &
      $(s,S)$-specific invariants: no order when IP $> s$;
      full order-up-to-$S$ when IP $\leq s$. \\
    \texttt{test\_periodic\_review} &
      Periodic-review policy only places orders on review
      days; silent on off-days. \\
    \texttt{test\_transport} &
      GreedyLoadPlanner vehicle packing; volume and weight
      dual constraints; tiered cost (full/half/quarter). \\
    \texttt{test\_multiroute\_costs} &
      Multi-mode lane: planner selects mode that minimises
      cost for the given load. \\
    \texttt{test\_multiroute\_share} &
      When multiple modes are available, load is split
      correctly across vehicles. \\
    \texttt{test\_routes\_and\_policies} &
      End-to-end test: correct policy applied per node/SKU
      combination as specified in config. \\
    \texttt{test\_system\_properties} &
      Aggregate system-level invariants (non-negative costs,
      fill rate in $[0,1]$, bullwhip ratio computed). \\
    \texttt{test\_finite\_supplier\_propagation} &
      Finite-capacity supplier correctly limits fulfilment;
      backlog propagates downstream. \\
    \texttt{test\_stochastic\_leadtime} &
      Simulator handles variable lead times drawn from a
      distribution without losing shipments. \\
    \texttt{test\_112\_timing} &
      Timing of receive-then-order within the daily tick
      for a 1-supplier, 1-warehouse, 2-retailer topology. \\
    \texttt{test\_variability} &
      Demand CV and order CV computed correctly; bullwhip
      metric is $\geq 1$ for base-stock policy under
      variable demand. \\
    \texttt{conftest} &
      Shared pytest fixtures: base config dict, simple
      1-supplier, 1-retailer network. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
