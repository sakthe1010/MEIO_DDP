\chapter{Test Suite Summary}
\label{appn:tests}

The framework is validated by 84 pytest tests organised
into 21 test modules.  Tests are run with \texttt{python -m pytest tests/ -q}.

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{Test Modules by Category}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{test\_deterministic\_constant\_demand} ---
        Base-stock convergence under constant demand; confirms on-hand
        reaches target within $L+1$ days.

  \item \textbf{test\_deterministic\_no\_demand} ---
        Simulator does not place orders or accumulate costs when demand
        is zero.

  \item \textbf{test\_zero\_lead\_time\_not\_lost} ---
        Goods ordered with lead time 0 arrive same-day; no units are
        silently dropped.

  \item \textbf{test\_negative\_demand\_clipped} ---
        Negative demand values are clipped to zero; inventory does not
        increase from demand.

  \item \textbf{test\_no\_negative\_on\_hand} ---
        On-hand inventory never goes below zero across all demand and
        policy configurations.

  \item \textbf{test\_costs\_and\_kpis} ---
        Holding, transport, ordering, and shortage costs accumulate
        correctly; warm-up exclusion verified.

  \item \textbf{test\_inventory\_recovery} ---
        After a demand spike, inventory recovers to base-stock within
        the expected number of lead-time periods.

  \item \textbf{test\_local\_flow\_conservation} ---
        Units entering the network equal units leaving plus units stored
        (flow conservation).

  \item \textbf{test\_policy\_behavior} ---
        Policy \texttt{order\_qty} returns correct values for each of
        the seven policy types across edge cases.

  \item \textbf{test\_ss\_policy\_behavior} ---
        $(s,S)$-specific invariants: no order when IP $> s$; full
        order-up-to-$S$ when IP $\leq s$.

  \item \textbf{test\_periodic\_review} ---
        Periodic-review policy only places orders on review days;
        silent on off-days.

  \item \textbf{test\_transport} ---
        GreedyLoadPlanner vehicle packing; volume and weight dual
        constraints; tiered cost (full/half/quarter).

  \item \textbf{test\_multiroute\_costs} ---
        Multi-mode lane: planner selects mode that minimises cost for
        the given load.

  \item \textbf{test\_multiroute\_share} ---
        When multiple modes are available, load is split correctly
        across vehicles.

  \item \textbf{test\_routes\_and\_policies} ---
        End-to-end test: correct policy applied per node/SKU combination
        as specified in config.

  \item \textbf{test\_system\_properties} ---
        Aggregate system-level invariants (non-negative costs, fill rate
        in $[0,1]$, bullwhip ratio computed).

  \item \textbf{test\_finite\_supplier\_propagation} ---
        Finite-capacity supplier correctly limits fulfilment; backlog
        propagates downstream.

  \item \textbf{test\_stochastic\_leadtime} ---
        Simulator handles variable lead times drawn from a distribution
        without losing shipments.

  \item \textbf{test\_112\_timing} ---
        Timing of receive-then-order within the daily tick for a
        1-supplier, 1-warehouse, 2-retailer topology.

  \item \textbf{test\_variability} ---
        Demand CV and order CV computed correctly; bullwhip metric is
        $\geq 1$ for base-stock policy under variable demand.

  \item \textbf{conftest} ---
        Shared pytest fixtures: base config dict, simple 1-supplier,
        1-retailer network.
\end{itemize}
